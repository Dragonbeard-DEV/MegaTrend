## What we collect

- **Contact form:** your name, company, email address, phone number and message.
- **Technical data:** information your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, used only to protect the form from abuse (rate limiting) and not stored.
- **Analytics:** only if you choose "Accept all" in the cookie banner. See the cookie policy.

## Why we use it

- To answer your request and discuss a possible collaboration. Legal basis: the consent you give when submitting the form.
- To keep this website secure.

## Where it goes

Form content is sent by email to our sales inbox through our email provider, Resend. The website itself does not store form content in a database.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries in our sales inbox for as long as needed to handle your request and meet our legal obligations.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by sending a request through the contact form on this website. You also have the rights provided under Vietnamese personal data protection law.
