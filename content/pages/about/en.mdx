## Who we are

Megatrend is a technology consultancy, design and system integration company, delivering ICT, ELV, Network Infrastructure, Smart Building and Hospitality technology solutions.

With a team of specialists bringing over 18 years of experience in technology and engineering infrastructure, we focus on solutions that work as one, scale with the business and meet the technical and operating standards of international hotel brands.

## Working with you

Megatrend works alongside clients throughout the project lifecycle — from technology strategy, system architecture design and solution selection to integration, deployment, testing and operational support.

We aim to build technology infrastructure that is reliable, secure and ready for the future, improving operational efficiency and maximising the value of our clients' investment.
