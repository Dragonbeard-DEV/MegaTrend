Megatrend provided independent supervision and technical consultancy for the ELV systems at Hilton Tapestry Casamia Hoi An in 2026, following our ELV and IT design review for the project in 2025.

## From drawings to site

The supervision aimed to ensure compliance with Hilton's operational standards, system reliability and a successful handover to the hotel operator.

## Systems covered

The scope included CCTV, access control, public address, audio-visual, background music, IPTV and PBX telephony. It also covered the building management system, including integration of HVAC, lighting control, pump control and energy monitoring, as well as supporting infrastructure.
