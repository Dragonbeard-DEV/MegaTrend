Amanoi Resort, in Vinh Hy Bay, has become the first Aman property globally to successfully deploy and operate a new cloud-based virtualization architecture, designed and implemented by the Megatrend engineering team.

## What the project covered

The work modernized the resort's technology platform through server virtualization, storage consolidation, a cloud migration strategy and modernization of its operational platform. It sits alongside a complete redesign of the resort's network infrastructure and ELV design and build for the resort's most luxurious villa cluster.

## What comes next

Our work at Amanoi continues with staff accommodation infrastructure, a high-availability firewall architecture and business continuity enhancements.
