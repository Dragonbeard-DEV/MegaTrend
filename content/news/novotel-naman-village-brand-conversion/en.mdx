Megatrend has completed its work on Novotel Naman Village in Da Nang, where an existing Citadines property was transformed into a Novotel-branded resort in full compliance with Accor's operational standards.

## Why brand conversions are different

A conversion starts with a building that already exists. Before anything can be designed, the team has to understand what is installed, what meets the new operator's requirements and what has to change.

## What we did

Our scope included complete ICT consultancy for Accor's requirements, infrastructure assessment and redesign, network transformation and ELV upgrade recommendations.

The conversion was approved, and the project was successfully completed and accepted by Accor. See the [project page](/en/projects/novotel-naman-village) for details.
