## Responsibilities

- Install and configure switches, firewalls, Wi-Fi and servers on project sites.
- Prepare network diagrams, test records and handover documents.
- Troubleshoot incidents and support hotel IT teams after handover.
- Work with project managers and vendors to keep deployments on schedule.

## Requirements

- Experience with enterprise networking and Wi-Fi; a certification such as CCNA is an advantage.
- Working knowledge of Windows Server and virtualization.
- Willingness to travel to project sites.
- Ability to read technical documents in English.

## What we offer

- Experience on projects for international hotel brands.
- Mentoring from a team with long experience in IT infrastructure projects.
- Training and certification support relevant to your role.
