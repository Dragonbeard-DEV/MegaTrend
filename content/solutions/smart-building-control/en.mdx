## Overview

A smart building is not a collection of apps. It is a control layer that lets lighting, air conditioning, curtains, pumps and energy meters share information and respond together. Getting there depends on open standards, a properly designed control backbone and careful commissioning.

For premium residences we build on KNX, an open international standard for home and building control, with devices available from many manufacturers. For larger buildings, a building management system (BMS) brings HVAC, lighting control, pump control and energy monitoring onto one operating view.

## Scope

- **Smart home:** consultancy, engineering design, implementation and commissioning of KNX platforms, as on the Panoma B4.2 penthouse in Da Nang.
- **BMS:** integration of HVAC, lighting, pumps and energy monitoring, including independent supervision for hotel projects.
- **Control infrastructure:** structured cabling, network and KNX backbone with room for expansion.
- **User interfaces:** scenes, touch panels and mobile control, including platforms such as Control4.

## How we work

1. **Understand daily use:** walk through how residents or operators will live with the system, room by room.
2. **Engineer:** produce the control design, device schedules and cabling requirements before walls close.
3. **Program and commission:** configure scenes and logic, then test every function on site.
4. **Hand over:** train users and leave complete project files, so the system remains maintainable.
