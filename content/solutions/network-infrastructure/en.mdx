## Overview

In a modern hotel or resort, the network is no longer just for internet access. Cameras, door locks, IPTV, telephones and building systems all depend on it. When it fails, the property feels it immediately, so the design has to account for separation between systems, redundancy and room to grow.

Megatrend designs new networks and modernizes existing ones. At Amanoi, for example, our scope included a complete redesign of the resort's network infrastructure, along with ongoing work on high-availability firewall architecture.

## Scope

- **Wired network:** core, distribution and access layers, sized for current and future systems.
- **Wireless network:** coverage planning, access point placement and configuration, on platforms such as Ruckus, HPE Aruba Networking and Cisco Meraki.
- **Fiber and GPON:** passive optical distribution where it suits the property's layout and scale.
- **Security:** firewalls, high-availability pairs and segmentation between guest, administrative and ELV networks.
- **Assessment:** review of existing infrastructure against an operator's requirements, with a redesign plan.

## How we work

1. **Assess:** map traffic, systems, buildings and any existing equipment.
2. **Design:** define the topology, segmentation, redundancy and addressing.
3. **Deploy:** supply, install and configure, with cutover planned around hotel operations.
4. **Validate:** test failover, Wi-Fi coverage and performance, then document the final configuration.
