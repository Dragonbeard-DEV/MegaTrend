## Overview

Active equipment gets replaced every few years, but cabling stays in the walls for much longer. A well-designed structured cabling system therefore protects the rest of the technology investment: new devices can be added or moved without opening up finished spaces, and faults can be traced quickly because every link is labelled and documented.

## Scope

- **Design:** backbone and horizontal cabling, outlet schedules and rack elevations, following recognized practice such as ANSI/TIA-568 and ISO/IEC 11801.
- **Pathways:** coordination of trays, conduits and risers with other disciplines.
- **Installation:** cable pulling, termination, patching and labelling.
- **Testing:** certification of copper and fiber links, with reports for each one.
- **Documentation:** as-built drawings and records that the operating team can use.

## How we work

1. **Plan:** count outlets, identify systems and allow spare capacity for future changes.
2. **Coordinate:** agree routes with the other trades before ceilings and walls close.
3. **Install:** terminate and label to the agreed scheme, keeping power and data properly separated.
4. **Certify:** test each link and hand over the results with the as-built drawings.
