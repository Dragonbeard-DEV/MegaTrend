## Background

The work covers the resort's ELV systems, its network and its data center, modernizing the technology platform of one of Vietnam's best-known luxury resorts.

## Scope of services

- **ELV design and build:** design and deployment of ELV systems for the resort's most luxurious villa cluster.
- **Network infrastructure transformation:** complete redesign and modernization of the resort network.
- **Data center and cloud transformation:** server virtualization architecture, storage consolidation, cloud migration strategy and operational platform modernization.

## Outcome

Amanoi became the first Aman property globally to successfully deploy and operate the new cloud-based virtualization architecture designed and implemented by our engineering team.

## Ongoing work

- Staff accommodation infrastructure development
- High-availability firewall architecture
- Business continuity enhancement
