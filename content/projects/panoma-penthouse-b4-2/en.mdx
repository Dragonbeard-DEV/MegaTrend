## Background

The penthouse, in one of Da Nang's most prestigious developments, called for a secure, intelligent and premium living environment, with infrastructure ready for future expansion.

## Scope of services

- **Smart home engineering and integration:** consultancy, engineering design, implementation and commissioning of a KNX-based platform.
- **ELV systems engineering:** end-to-end design and deployment of integrated ELV systems.
- **ICT and control infrastructure design:** structured cabling, network infrastructure and the KNX control backbone.
