## Background

The objective was to transform the existing Citadines property into a Novotel-branded resort while ensuring full compliance with Accor's operational standards.

## Scope of services

- ICT and ELV consulting
- System assessment and technical compliance
- Technology transformation
- Design and build, and equipment supply
- Infrastructure assessment and redesign
- Network transformation and ELV upgrade recommendations

## Outcome

The brand conversion was approved, and the project was successfully completed and accepted by Accor.
