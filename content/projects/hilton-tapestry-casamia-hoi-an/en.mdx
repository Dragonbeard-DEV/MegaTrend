## Background

We provided an independent technical view, first on the design and then on the ELV systems, to ensure compliance with Hilton's operational standards, system reliability and a successful handover to the hotel operator.

## Scope of services

- **ELV and IT design review (2025):** design verification, system integration and technical compliance across CCTV, public address, access control, PMS, structured cabling, BMS-related infrastructure, servers, and common infrastructure such as UPS, racks, cable trays and grounding.
- **Independent ELV supervision (2026):** supervision and technical consultancy across CCTV, access control, public address, audio-visual, background music, IPTV and PBX.
- **Building management system:** including integration of HVAC, lighting control, pump control and energy monitoring.
- **Supporting infrastructure:** cable trays, conduits, containment and associated power distribution.
