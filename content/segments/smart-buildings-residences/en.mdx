## The challenge

Owners of premium residences and mixed-use buildings expect lighting, climate, security and connectivity to simply work together. That is hard to achieve if each system is added separately late in the project, because the cabling and control backbone have to be in place before interiors are finished, and the systems have to remain serviceable for years.

## Our approach

We engineer ICT, ELV and control infrastructure as one design, using open platforms such as KNX and BMS integration. On the Panoma B4.2 penthouse in Da Nang, for example, we handle consultancy, engineering design, implementation and commissioning of a KNX-based smart home, along with its integrated ELV systems.
