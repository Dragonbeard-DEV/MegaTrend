## The challenge

A hotel runs around the clock, and its technology has to do the same. Each international operator has its own technical standard, covering everything from guest internet to back-office servers. Projects have to satisfy those standards, stay on the construction programme and hand over systems that the hotel team can actually run. For existing properties, upgrades or brand conversions add the challenge of working with what is already installed.

## Our approach

With over 18 years of industry experience, our team has delivered consultancy, design, design review and technical supervision for hospitality projects operated by Hilton, Hyatt, Marriott, Accor, IHG, Wyndham, Radisson and Aman. We use that experience to plan early, translate brand standards into practical designs and stay involved through commissioning and operational support.
